Your data, explained.

Information stored on your device

Orbit stores notes, tasks, workspace/project information, chat history, preferences, layouts, and other saved tool data in a local database. User-configured folder watches may record local file-activity summaries. System displays read local CPU, memory, disk, and process information; game and foreground behavior may inspect the active application.

Optional clipboard history stores text locally and is off by default. Its retention and exclusion controls cannot guarantee that every copied secret is excluded. It is not secure password storage.

The database and ordinary exports are not encrypted by Orbit. Their protection depends on your Windows account, device security, and disk encryption. Local warning/error logs may also be written. Do not share logs without checking and removing private information.

Provider API keys are stored through Windows Credential Manager rather than in the ordinary database or distributed app package. The Store build uses separate data and credential namespaces and does not automatically import desktop-version keys. Complete packaged credential and migration testing remains required.

Microphone, wake phrase, and AI services

Voice features use the microphone when you start them. Optional wake-phrase setup saves enrollment audio and matching features locally. The wake helper matches locally and does not upload its background audio or profile. Once explicitly enabled after checks, it listens in the background and pauses during an active voice conversation or Game Mode. Turning listening off does not delete saved or archived enrollment clips.

During Gemini Live sessions, microphone audio and conversation information are sent to Google. Orbit retains a bounded live transcript in memory rather than recording live-session audio to a file. Groq text chat sends conversation context needed for a reply; Groq dictation sends captured audio; optional Orpheus speech sends reply text to Groq. Windows speech uses installed Windows voices. Stopping a session cannot retract information already sent.

These providers process requests under their own terms and account settings. Free-tier eligibility, quotas, and data use may differ from paid services. Review Google's Gemini API terms, Google's privacy policy, and Groq's privacy policy, services agreement, and data-processing terms before sending sensitive content. Orbit supplies no shared developer-funded key and cannot verify a project's billing state from an API key alone.

Current Gemini and Groq API terms require adult professional/developer use. Google also requires paid services when API clients are made available in the EEA, Switzerland, or UK. Orbit's free-only public AI availability and setup controls remain under review; an API key does not override provider eligibility rules. This site does not promise worldwide free AI access.

Orbit has no dedicated advertising or analytics backend and does not automatically upload your workspace. Explicitly opening a link or launcher uses the corresponding external app or site. Windows, Microsoft Store, and WebView2 may perform their own platform operations under their settings.

Information you send to support

Reports are voluntary. Opening the support page sends no notes, transcript, recording, or diagnostic attachment to the publisher. If you send an email, the publisher and email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. That information is used to investigate and respond to your request. Do not send credentials or unnecessary personal information.

The support mailbox uses Microsoft Outlook. Microsoft processes email under its privacy statement. You may ask about or request deletion of support correspondence through the same support address. Retention and any legal requirements will be explained in response; no fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Removing local app data does not delete mail already sent to support or information held by AI providers.

This support website

The page code includes no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, account login, contact-form backend, or third-party font/image requests. These static pages are prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, under its privacy statement. Its hosting operations are separate from Orbit's local workspace storage.

Choosing an external provider link opens a different website with its own privacy practices. An email link opens your chosen email app; it does not send a message automatically.

Your choices and retention

You can control microphone sessions, wake listening, clipboard history, watched folders, saved credentials, and startup settings in Orbit. Ordinary local data remains until you change or remove it. Backups, exports, archived clips, and Credential Manager entries can be separate copies.

Do not assume uninstalling removes every copy or credential. Complete deletion/export and uninstall behavior must be verified for the released package. Contact support for the steps appropriate to your version before deleting folders. The publisher cannot remotely inspect or erase your local workspace through this website.

This notice will be updated when the shipped behavior or support arrangements change. Contact the publisher through the support channel above with privacy questions.
