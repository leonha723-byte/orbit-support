Contact Orbit support

Use this channel for app problems, privacy questions, accessibility feedback, or concerning AI output. Support is provided by the independent publisher, Leon23; a fixed response time is not guaranteed.

Email Orbit support

Or copy this address into your email app: orbitcommandcenter.leon23@outlook.com.

Never send API keys, passwords, enrollment recordings, databases, or your full chat history. Start with a short description. You decide whether to send any redacted screenshot or excerpt.

For a bug report, include

  • The Orbit version and your Windows version.
  • What you tried, what happened, and what you expected.
  • The exact error wording, with keys, file paths, names, and private content removed.

Report unsafe or inappropriate AI output

Use the support address above with the subject Orbit AI output report. Include the provider, the approximate time, a brief description of your concern, and only the minimum redacted excerpt you choose to share. An excerpt is optional.

Sending a report does not automatically contact Google or Groq, and this site cannot remove content from their systems. You can also use the relevant provider's own reporting tools. AI responses may be inaccurate; verify important information before relying on it.

Quick answers

How do I arrange the HUD?

Drag a group circle to move it; click it to show or hide its panels. Drag a panel by its move area to position it independently. Panels may overlap. Tidy rearranges the layout, so use it only when you want that change.

How do I use the voice orb?

Click the orb to start. With Gemini Live, speak and pause for its reply; clicking again ends the session. Voice needs microphone permission, a supported provider setup, and network access. Wake-phrase activation is optional and requires its own setup and checks.

Is AI free and unlimited?

No. Orbit's core tools do not need an AI subscription, but optional providers have their own eligibility, terms, quotas, and billing rules. An API key cannot prove billing is disabled. Keep billing disabled in the provider project if you intend to use only its free tier; Orbit must not enable it for you.

Why is Orbit still running after I close its window?

The window close button may hide Orbit to the system tray. To exit completely, open the tray menu and choose Quit Orbit. Save any in-progress work before exiting or updating.

How do I turn off background wake listening?

Turn off wake listening in Orbit's voice settings or tray controls. This stops the listener; it does not itself delete saved phrase recordings. Do not delete app folders while Orbit is running.

How do I handle a Windows security block?

Keep Smart App Control enabled. Record the error and contact support using the channel above. A name reservation or a private test package does not mean an app has passed Store certification.
